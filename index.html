Institutional Mandate

Operating with Unconditional Humanity & Neutrality

As part of the International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement, our mandate is to prevent and alleviate human suffering in the face of disasters, health emergencies, and armed conflicts without discrimination as to nationality, race, religious beliefs, class, or political opinions.

Guiding Ethics

The 7 Fundamental Principles

01
Humanity

Preventing and alleviating human suffering wherever found.

02
Impartiality

No discrimination as to race, nationality, or political views.

03
Neutrality

Maintaining public trust by not taking sides in armed conflicts.

04
Independence

Autonomy from governmental and political pressure.

05
Voluntary

Prompted by desire to serve, never by gain or profit.

06
Unity

One National Society per nation, open to all citizens.

07
Universality

Worldwide solidarity in sharing equal responsibilities.

Core Capabilities

Humanitarian Relief Programs

Engineered for immediate disaster intervention, rapid logistical airlift, and long-term community resilience.

Emergency Health & Trauma Units

Deployable surgical field hospitals, trauma triage backpacks, mobile maternal clinics, and mass outbreak vaccination teams ready within 24 hours.

  • WHO EMT Class 2 certified
  • Emergency blood delivery & cold chain

WASH: Water & Hygiene

Rapid purification units delivering up to 600,000 liters of safe drinking water per day, flood latrines, and cholera containment protocols.

  • Solar high-volume desalinators
  • Dignity & hygiene kit distribution

Emergency Shelter & NFIs

Pre-positioned heavy duty tarpaulins, winterized all-weather family tents, thermal blankets, solar lanterns, and cooking sets for displaced populations.

  • Rapid kit airlift via strategic hubs
  • Emergency transition housing solutions

Food Security & Cash Assistance

Combating acute hunger with ready-to-use therapeutic food (RUTF), biometric cash distribution systems, and local market revival programs.

  • Dignity-first digital cash vouchers
  • Severely malnourished infant therapy

Emergency Telecommunications

Deploying portable satellite uplinks, mesh radio networks, and disaster connectivity pods so responders and separated families can reconnect.

  • Restoring Family Links (RFL) network
  • Field drone mapping for search & rescue

Protection, Gender & Inclusion

Safeguarding vulnerable children, women, elderly, and persons with disabilities in disaster zones against exploitation and trauma.

  • Child-friendly safe spaces in camps
  • Community psychosocial first aid
